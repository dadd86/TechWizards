Help center

How to play

  • Pick a number from 1 to 6 and roll the dice.
  • Open the history screen to review your recent games and results.
  • You can enable or disable chip animations and sounds from Settings.

Sound and music

  • Background music runs as a foreground service and honors calls or alarms.
  • Toggle the official soundtrack or select a custom track from your device media.
  • Sound effects play together with chip movements when animations are on.

Notifications and celebrations

  • When you win, a notification links directly to the calendar event created for the victory.
  • The app may capture your winning screen and store it in the gallery for easy sharing.
  • If notifications are disabled in Settings, celebrations stay within the current screen.

Privacy and location

  • Winning locations are stored locally in SQLite only for in-game features.
  • No personal data is sent outside the app and logs avoid sensitive information.

Accessibility and languages

  • The interface is localized in Spanish, English, and German; choose your language in Settings.
  • The help WebView loads local content with scripts disabled to keep the experience safe.
